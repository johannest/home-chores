# Mobile UX review — 16 Sep 2026

Scope: phone users, all family sizes and chore-list sizes, low vision, imprecise touch.
Method: full read of the UI code and `styles.css`, then hands-on testing of the app on the
in-memory verify server with a seeded home of 5 members (one with the 19-character name
"Alexander-Sebastian") and 30 chores in 4 groups. Tested at 375×812, 320×568, 812×375
(landscape), 375 at 150% browser text size, dark mode, Finnish, as admin and as a child
member. Contrast ratios were measured from computed styles; touch targets from bounding
boxes with `pointer: coarse` active.

Screen-reader and keyboard findings are recorded at the end but **deferred**: the app is
used almost entirely by touch on phones.

## Status — 18 Sep 2026

All ten items in the list below were implemented. Re-measured on the same seeded home
(5 members, 30 chores, admin, English) after the change:

| Scenario | Header | First chore card top | Tabs fit? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 375×812 | 174 → 67 px | 782 → 500 px | no → yes (335 of 359 px) |
| 320×568 | 219 → 67 px | 854 → 554 px | no → yes (280 of 304 px) |
| 375×812, text 150% | 301 → 97 px | 1273 → 682 px | no → yes |

What changed: header actions folded into one "⋯" menu (`HeaderMenu`, with appearance and
language as sub menus); main tabs share the row with icon above label below 640 px;
leaderboard goes compact (face + count + credits, name in tooltip) from 4 members; filter
chips are one horizontally scrolling row; card badges use a shortened first name and never
wrap; "I'll do it", Undo, snooze chip and filter chips are 40 px targets with more gap under
the tap area; admin Chores card folds per group with a per-row "⋯" menu, Members rows use the
same menu, and Home settings is split into "Chore rules" and "Home settings"; stats bar labels
sit above the bar on phones and chart text is 11–12 px; deleting a list item shows a
"Removed … — Undo" toast instead of deleting silently.

Still open from this review: the contrast items (A, B and the table below) and the deferred
screen-reader/keyboard section.

## Top phone-specific issues to fix first

1. **The first chore card is below the fold.** Header, tabs, daily ring, leaderboard and
   filter chips come first. At 375×812 the first card starts at 782 px; at 320×568 at
   854 px (1.5 screens); in landscape at 612 px; in Finnish at 888 px; at 150% text at
   1273 px. Everything below in items 2–5 contributes to this one.
2. **The Admin tab scrolls out of view.** Four icon+label tabs need 406 px (450 px in
   Finnish) but get 359 px at 375 wide and 304 px at 320. Only a chevron hints that a
   fourth tab exists. `HomeView.buildTabs`. Fix: icon-only tabs below ~480 px, or a bottom
   navigation bar.
3. **The header is 3–5 rows tall on phones** (174 px at 375, 219 px at 320, 301 px at
   150% text). Palette, language, bell, Admin?/Leave and invite are rarely used. Fix: one
   "⋯" menu for the rare actions, leaving title + one row. `HomeView.buildHeader`,
   `.home-header` rules in `styles.css`.
4. **The leaderboard grows one row per member**: 5 chips take 200 px; a long name fills a
   row alone. A one-row version was tried and reverted (commits 4490b2d / 4f17698). Fix
   candidates: compact avatar+count chips when there are 4+ members, or a collapsible
   section like "Done today". `ChoresPanel.renderLeaderboard`, `.leaderboard`.
5. **Filter chips wrap to 3 rows (4 in Finnish)** — 116 px / 156 px. Fix: a horizontally
   scrolling chip strip, or "Today / All / More…" with cadence chips behind a sheet.
   `ChoresPanel.renderFilters`, `.filter-bar`.
6. **Long member names break card badges.** "Alexander-Sebastian ×3 🔒" wraps to three
   lines (60 px) and overlays the emoji; "🕒 8–10, 18–22" wraps to two. `.task-card
   .streak` has a max-width but wraps instead of truncating. Fix: `white-space: nowrap`
   + ellipsis, and a first-name/initials fallback above ~10 characters (also helps the
   leaderboard). `styles.css` `.task-card .streak`, `ChoresPanel.badgeText`.
7. **"I'll do it" sits 6 px under the tap-to-complete area and is only 30 px tall**
   (LUMO_SMALL). A slightly-off tap completes instead of books, or vice versa. Undo (board
   strip and celebration dialog) is also 30 px; the snooze chip is 28×39 px; filter chips
   36 px. Fix: 40–44 px targets and more separation between complete and book.
   `ChoresPanel.taskCard`, `.book-btn`, `.snooze-chip`.
8. **Admin panel does not scale to 30 chores or 5 members.** Chores card is 2434 px tall,
   every row wraps its four buttons under the name (65–121 px per row), reordering by
   ↑/↓ across 30 items is dozens of taps; member rows are 125 px each; Home settings is one
   1814 px card with 15 controls. Fix: collapsible per-group sub-sections in Chores, a
   per-row "⋯" menu for edit/delete/move, and split settings into 2–3 cards.
   `AdminPanel.choresSection`, `membersSection`, `settingsSection`.
9. **Stats are hard to read on a phone.** Bar labels get a 74 px column, so chore names
   truncate to "Recycli…", "Descal…". Stat-tile labels are 9.9 px, day-trend labels 9.6 px,
   week/month labels 8.8 px. Fix: label above bar below 640 px; nothing under 12 px.
   `.bar-row` (mobile), `.stat-label`, `.trend-day`, `.trend-wide .trend-day`.
10. **Deleting a list item is one tap with no confirm or undo**, and the trash icon is at
    the row edge where thumbs land. `ListPanel.itemRow`.

Not phone-specific but cheap and high-value: the brand text token (item A below) and the
header text contrast (item B).

Other observations:
- Card grid is 2 columns down to 320 px; a 3-line chore name stretches its whole grid row.
- Group heading text wraps fine; group names are clipped to 30 chars at input.
- Dark mode holds up; card borders at 10% contrast are faint but readable.
- Add-chore dialog fits and scrolls internally at 375; the hours placeholder looks like a
  filled value.

## Low-vision findings (contrast, text size)

WCAG AA: 4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large text and icons. Light mode; dark mode in brackets.

| Element | Ratio | Where |
|---|---|---|
| A. Brand text colour: selected tab, selected chip, daily ring, "I'll do it", "Add chore" | 4.2 | `--lumo-primary-text-color` light value `hsl(155,62%,34%)`; 30% lightness gives 5.2 |
| B. White header text on gradient (title, chore master, Admin badge, Leave, code chip) | 2.6–2.9 | `--app-gradient`; darken, add text shadow, or dark text on light pills |
| Snooze ⏰ chip at opacity 0.35 | 2.0 [2.5] | `.snooze-chip` |
| Locked card name at opacity 0.55 | 3.3 [4.1] | `.task-card.locked` — dim the surface, not the text |
| Locked card badge | 2.2 | same |
| Credit chip "💎 27" | 3.6 | `.member-chip .credit-chip` |
| White initials on member colours lime/amber/teal/green/sky/orange | 2.0–2.8 | `MemberAvatar.dot`; red/pink 3.5–3.8, violet/indigo 4.2–4.5 |

Text sizes: stat tile labels 9.9 px, day-trend labels 9.6 px, week/month trend labels
8.8 px, card badges 11.5 px, `.sub` lines 12.5 px, filter chips 12.8 px.

## Bugs found along the way

- Restoring your own backup shows the toast "This home was deleted by an admin." The
  HomeState revision bump reaches the still-attached board (`HomeView.buildChrome` →
  `showOut`) before the restore toast.
- After switching the UI to Finnish (which reloads the page), `<html lang>` stays `en`.
- The language Select has no visible or accessible label.
- When "require approval" is on, an admin's own completion shows "Sent for approval" and
  the admin approves themself. Possibly intended; reads oddly.

## Deferred: screen reader and keyboard

- Chore tap areas, filter chips, member chips, snooze chips and celebration feedback
  buttons are plain `div`s with no role, tabindex or name. The stat tiles in `StatsPanel`
  already set role/tabindex/aria-pressed and are the template.
- Icon-only buttons without aria-label: member rename/remove, chore delete, group delete,
  spree-tier delete in `AdminPanel`.

## Reproducing

Run the `flashchores-verify` launch config (in-memory H2, port 8081), create a home, then
restore a backup JSON whose `home.code` matches, with 5 members and 30 tasks, via Admin →
Backup & restore. Restore signs the device out; sign back in via Join → "I'm already a
member" with a member name and the PIN from the backup file.
