⚡ FlashChores

FlashChores is a free, effortless chore tracker for families. Create a home, share a short code with your family, and tap a chore when you do it — the board keeps things fair, tracks streaks and credits, and celebrates together with you. No accounts, no ads, and nicknames instead of real names. Works on any phone as an installable app, in English, Finnish and Swedish.
